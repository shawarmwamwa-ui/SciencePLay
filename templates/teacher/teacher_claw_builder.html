Claw Machine Cabinet Rule: 2-Chute Target Matching

The physical claw machine arcade stage features 2 drop zones (Left Chute vs Right Chute). If your science lesson covers 3+ states or concepts (such as Solid, Liquid, and Gas), frame this sorting game as a focused comparison (e.g., Solid vs. Liquid, or Solids vs. Non-Solids).
